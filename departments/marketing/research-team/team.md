# Research Team

What this team owns: knowing what's happening outside your business. What competitors ship, what's working in your space, what belongs in your marketing brain. Everything it learns lands in `knowledge/`, never straight into your copy.

## Members
- **Researcher** (`researcher/`): competitors, swipes, and posts beating their own channel size.

## Workflow: run my marketing checkup
Say: "run my marketing checkup"
Also say: how's my marketing doing, monthly review, what's working right now, check my competitors
When: Once a month. Steps whose setup isn't ready yet (no key, no stats) get skipped with a plain line saying so. A checkup that silently skips half its checks is lying.
### Outside
1. **Researcher** · competitor-check · what changed out there.
2. **Researcher** · find-outliers · what's overperforming in your space.
### Inside
3. **Traffic Agent** · weekly-traffic-read · what came to your site, from where, and one action.
4. **Social Manager** · read-the-stats · how each post did against its own job.
### Report
5. **Researcher** writes `knowledge/reports/marketing-checkup-<YYYY-MM>.md` from the four steps above: competitors, outliers, traffic, content, and the ONE thing to do next. Anything that changes what a team is building gets flagged to you directly.

## Memory
Habits every team member on this team should share land here. A correction about one team member goes in that member's own file instead.
