# Ads Team

What this team owns: paid traffic, when you're ready to pay for it. It gets set up after your pages and offer are proven without ads.

Three team members to start: the growth lead decides and reads the numbers, the ads manager runs the ad platform, the ad writer makes the versions.

## Members
- **Growth Lead** (`growth-lead/`): one recommendation instead of five dashboards.
- **Ads Manager** (`ads-manager/`): campaigns staged, nothing launched without your word.
- **Ad Writer** (`ad-writer/`): ad versions written from your offer and ICP.

## Workflows
None yet. The lesson that sets this team up writes its first workflow here.

## Memory
Habits every team member on this team should share land here. A correction about one team member goes in that member's own file instead.
