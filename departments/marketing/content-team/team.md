# Content Team

What this team owns: everything you publish. The words, the visuals, the calendar, the numbers after.

Four team members to start, and that's enough. The copywriter writes, the social manager runs where it all goes, the editor is the second pass and keeps your voice, the designer makes it look like you. Specialists grow later, one at a time, when a platform earns one.

## Members
- **Copywriter** (`copywriter/`): writes posts, captions and page words in your voice.
- **Editor** (`editor/`): the second pass on everything, and the keeper of `brand/voice.md`.
- **Social Manager** (`social-manager/`): your content mix, the calendar, posting, and the numbers.
- **Designer** (`designer/`): visuals and shareable documents in your colors and fonts.

## Workflow: make a post
Say: "make a post"
Also say: write me a post, I need a post, draft a post for this week
When: Any time you want one post written, checked and ready for you to send.
### Plan
1. **Social Manager** reads `brand/channels.md` and picks the job this post does (give, prove or invite) and where it goes.
### Write
2. **Copywriter** · draft-post · drafts it in your voice for one buyer (a persona from `brand/personas/` if you have them).
3. **Copywriter** · write-hooks · writes the first line that earns the rest.
### Check
4. **Editor** · edit-a-draft · the second pass against `brand/voice.md`.
5. **You** read the draft and say yes or what to fix. [you approve]
### Post
6. **Social Manager** · post-content · stages it on its platform for your final send and logs it.

## Workflow: plan my week of content
Say: "plan my week of content"
Also say: plan my posts, what should I post this week, make my content calendar, content for this week
When: Once a week. It stops first if `brand/icp.md`, `brand/voice.md` or Where You Show Up in `brand/channels.md` is still empty, because the content-brain skill fills that and a week planned without it is posting to post.
### Read
1. **Social Manager** · read-the-stats · last week's numbers and which openers won. Skipped on the very first week.
### Plan
2. **Social Manager** · plan-the-week · picks 3 to 5 posts from `knowledge/topics.md`, each with its job, its one reader and its platform, and one that goes out today. [you approve]
### Write
3. **Copywriter** · draft-post · drafts each one, with something useful inside the post, not only an ask at the end.
4. **Copywriter** · write-hooks · five first lines for each post, from the opener bank and your winners in `knowledge/hooks.md`. You pick.
### Check
5. **Editor** · edit-a-draft · the second pass against `brand/voice.md`, read out loud. [you approve]
### Post
6. **Social Manager** · post-content · stages today's post for your send, puts the rest on the calendar on their days, and logs each one.

## Workflow: make a short video script
Say: "make a short video script"
Also say: write me a reel, script a video, I want to record something, video idea
When: Any time you want to record. Best after "plan my week of content" picks the topic.
### Write
1. **Copywriter** · write-a-script · two or three options on different openers and structures, each one opening a question in the first line and answering it at the end.
2. **Editor** · edit-a-draft · reads each option out loud and cuts whatever stalls. [you approve]
### Record
3. **You** record it in one take on your phone, reading from the script, and drop the file in `recordings/`.
### Post
4. **Designer** · make-a-cover · the cover in your one locked style.
5. **Social Manager** · post-content · stages it with its caption for your send and logs which opener it used.

## Platform specialists
When a single platform earns its own attention, tell your AI "stand up my [platform] specialist" and it builds the folder under this team from the pattern in `platform-specialists.md`. Don't pre-create them.

## Memory
Habits every team member on this team should share land here. A correction about one team member goes in that member's own file instead.
