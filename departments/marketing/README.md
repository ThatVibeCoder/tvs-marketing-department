# Marketing

Everything that gets you known and chosen: content, video editing, your website, research, follow-up, newsletter and ads.

Each folder in here will be an agent team. Each team has a `team.md` (its members and its workflows) and one folder per team member.

This department starts empty on purpose. Its teams arrive from the classroom lessons: the skill doc at the bottom of each lesson builds one team in here from your own answers, then runs the sync command (`python3 tools/sync.py`, or `py tools/sync.py` on Windows).
