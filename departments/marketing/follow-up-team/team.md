# Follow-Up Team

What this team owns: everything that happens after somebody already knows you. Replies, check-ins, the message that keeps a lead warm.

Waiting on purpose. Your company strategist decides when this team gets set up, based on your business audit.

## Members
- **Reply Agent** (`reply-agent/`): drafts replies in your voice. You send.
- **Nurture Agent** (`nurture-agent/`): keeps warm leads warm and flags anyone going quiet.

## Workflows
None yet. The lesson or brief that sets this team up writes its first workflow here, in the same shape as the other teams.

## Memory
Habits every team member on this team should share land here. A correction about one team member goes in that member's own file instead.
