# Example Team

**This is a demo.** It's here so you can see how a team is put together before you add a real one. Every real team follows the same shape: this file with its header, members and workflows, a folder for each team member, and each member's skills inside it. Delete this folder whenever you like; nothing depends on it.

## Members
- **Example Member** (`example-member/`): shows you around your department and adds teams from the library.

## Workflow: show me around
Say: "show me around"
Also say: what's in my department, what teams can I add, give me the tour
When: Your first day, or any time you want to see what's here and what you can add next.
### Look
1. **Example Member** · tour-the-department · shows what's in your department now and every team waiting in the library.
### Add
2. **You** pick the team the classroom is on, or say not yet. [you approve]
3. **Example Member** adds that whole team from the library and runs the sync command, so it shows up in the Command Center.

## Memory
Habits every team member on this team should share land here. A correction about one team member goes in that member's own file instead.
