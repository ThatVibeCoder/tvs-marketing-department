# Strategy Team

What this team owns: the business itself. It knows everything in your repo, keeps your shared brain true, scores your ideas, and picks what the company does next. It doesn't make content. It's the team you ask questions.

Every team member here starts seeded. The classroom sets each one up: your business files fill in first, then the strategist, then the rest.

## Members
- **Company Strategist** (`company-strategist/`): reads the whole repo, answers questions about the business, and writes the brief for what comes next.
- **ICP & Offer Owner** (`icp-offer-owner/`): owns who you serve and what you sell: `brand/icp.md`, `brand/personas/`, `brand/friction-points.md`, `brand/contrarian-take.md` and `brand/offer.md`.
- **Moat Analyst** (`moat-analyst/`): scores your business, or any idea, out of 40. Only runs when you ask.
- **Notion Assistant** (`notion-assistant/`) (optional): joins here only if you run part of your business in Notion. If you don't, it never gets made.

## Workflow: what comes next
Say: "what comes next"
Also say: what should I do next, what's my next move, which team member should I set up next
When: Any time you want the strategist's one pick for the next move, usually at the end of a week. Needs the Set Up Your Company Strategist lesson done first.
### Read
1. **Company Strategist** · company-strategist · reads the whole repo and says what's true, what's thin, and what's missing, then makes ONE pick with its reasons.
### Pick
2. **You** push back where it's wrong, then agree on the pick. [you approve]
### Brief
3. **Company Strategist** writes the brief to `departments/operations/strategy-team/company-strategist/briefs/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<team-member>.md`, in the shape `briefs/README.md` gives.

To set up the rest of this team and wake the team member the strategist picked, run the speed-run-your-team skill (the Speed Run a Team Member lesson installs it).

## Memory
Habits every team member on this team should share land here. A correction about one team member goes in that member's own file instead.
