# IT / Systems

The machine under everything: repo, live site, database, keys and the checks that keep them safe.

Each folder in here is an agent team. Each team has a `team.md` (its members and its workflows) and one folder per team member.

The Dev Team (`dev-team/`) ships here on Day 1: it's the plumbing every other team needs (saving your work to GitHub, your keys, connecting your tools, shipping your pages). Summon it with `/dev-team`.
