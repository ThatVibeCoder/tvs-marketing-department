# Swipes

**Filled by:** the Research Team lesson: its Researcher files a swipe whenever you send something that caught your eye.

One file per swipe: `<YYYY-MM-DD>-<short-name>.md` (copy `_template.md`): what it is, where it came from, why it works, the shape worth stealing, and its job (hook, offer, proof or structure). The job tag is how a writer finds "I need a proof section" later.
