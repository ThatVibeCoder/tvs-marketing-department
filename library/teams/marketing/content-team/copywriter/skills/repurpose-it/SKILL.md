# Repurpose It

One good piece of content holds more than one post. This takes a video the owner already made, or a post that already won, and turns it into four more pieces for the same week, so a winner gets used before a brand-new idea gets written. The owner walks out with four drafts ready for the week.

## Read first
- The source: a video's note in `recordings/` (with its script and transcript) or its edit notes in `departments/marketing/editing-team/video-editor/output/`, or a winning post from the newest `knowledge/reports/content-*.md`. Use what was actually SAID or posted, never the plan for it.
- `brand/voice.md`, `brand/icp.md`, `brand/channels.md` (Where You Show Up: only write for the platforms the owner posts on) and The Four Questions in `knowledge/topics.md`.
- The opener bank in write-hooks and `knowledge/hooks.md`.

## Say this first
Tell the owner, in your own plain words: "This video already did the hard part. I'm turning it into four more pieces for this week, so you're not starting from a blank page every time."

## The four pieces
Each one answers The Four Questions, gets its own first line from the opener bank (a different shape from the source's), and keeps the source's one useful thing:
1. **A caption post** for platform 1 in Where You Show Up: the video's point in writing, shaped by that platform's specialist if one exists.
2. **A carousel outline:** a hook slide, one idea per slide, a save-or-send last slide. It goes to the Designer's make-a-carousel when the owner wants it built.
3. **A newsletter paragraph:** one short section for their email list, written like a note from them, ending on one question people can reply to. Skip it if they have no list, and say so.
4. **A question post:** one question for their audience that the video makes them want to answer ("Which of these three is your roof doing?").
Never add a claim, a number or a story the source didn't have. If a piece needs one, leave it out of that piece.

## Three examples (made-up businesses from the other lessons; never copy them)
- **Marcus's attic video** → a Facebook group post with the 2-minute check · a carousel "Wet wood or dry ring? 5 photos" · a paragraph for his past-customer email · "Have you ever had a stain turn out to be nothing?"
- **Priya's "skip the website" video** → a LinkedIn post on the three referral partners · a carousel of the 4 questions · a paragraph for her list · "Who sent you your first client?"
- **Hannah's tunneling video** → an Instagram caption post · a carousel "Fix a tunneled candle in 3 steps" · a paragraph in her October email · "What's the scent you burn the most?"

## Where it goes
`departments/marketing/content-team/copywriter/output/repurpose-<YYYY-MM-DD>-<short-name>.md`: the four pieces, each labeled with its platform and its job (give / prove / invite). They join the week through the Social Manager's plan-the-week, and the source's topic line in `knowledge/topics.md` gets a note: "repurposed into 4, <date>".

## The ending, always
1. Show the four, one at a time. ⛔ The owner keeps, fixes or cuts each one.
2. Hand the keepers to the Editor's edit-a-draft, then to plan-the-week to put them on the calendar.
3. Save the work with the GitHub Agent's save-work skill (`departments/it-systems/dev-team/github-agent/skills/save-work/SKILL.md`), and tell the owner in one line what changed.

## Done when
Four pieces exist from one source, each answers The Four Questions in the owner's voice, nothing in them was invented, and the owner kept, fixed or cut each one.
