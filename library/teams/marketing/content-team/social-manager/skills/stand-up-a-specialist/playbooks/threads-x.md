# Threads and X playbook

Read by stand-up-a-specialist when the owner picked Threads or X. Both are fast, short and conversational.

## How they work
- **One thought per post.** A sentence or two that stands on its own. If it needs a paragraph to make sense, it belongs on LinkedIn or in an email.
- **Replies are the reach.** Answering other people's posts in your space gets you seen by their followers, so a daily habit of a few real replies does more than a perfect post.
- **Links go in a reply,** not the post, because a link in the first post is shown to fewer people.
- **Volume is normal here.** One to three posts a day is a lot on other platforms and ordinary here, but start at one a day until the workflow runs on its own.

## What to post first
Five short posts from `knowledge/topics.md`, each one a single opinion, tip or number, plus ten real replies to people your buyer follows.

## The rules
- Write it the way you'd say it out loud, and cut every word that isn't doing work.
- No threads of ten posts in week one. One good line beats ten okay ones.
- Your contrarian take does well here. A sentence people want to agree or argue with is the whole format.

## Three examples (made-up businesses, never copy them)
- Marcus, the roofer: "Most roof leaks we get called about are one cracked boot around a pipe. Not a roof."
- Priya, who coaches therapists: "You don't need a website before your first private client. You need three referral partners."
- Hannah, the candle maker: "If your candle tunnels, the first burn was too short. Every time."
