# Instagram playbook

Read by stand-up-a-specialist when the owner picked Instagram. The specialist's file gets the parts below, rewritten for THIS owner's business.

## How it decides who sees what
- **Reels** go to people who don't follow you yet. That's the format built for strangers. Instagram pushes reels under about 90 seconds to new people; long ones mostly stay with followers.
- **Carousels** go to your followers first. They reach new people when someone saves them or sends them to a friend in a DM. Sends count most.
- **Single photos** reach the fewest people of any format. Use one only when there's no video and no carousel.
- **Stories** are for people who already know you: polls, questions, behind the scenes, a link. They keep people warm, they don't find new ones.

## What to post in week one
One reel (a phone one-take is fine) and one carousel. The reel finds strangers, the carousel gives your followers something to save.

## The rules
- **The first second and the first slide do all the work.** A reel's opening line is said in the first two seconds. A carousel's first slide is its cover, and every other slide is cropped to its size, so make slide 1 the hook at 4:5 (1080 × 1350).
- **The caption's first line is the second hook.** Only about a line shows before "more".
- **Ask for the save or the send, not the like.** "Send this to someone who..." does more than "like if...".
- **3 to 5 posts a week at most** while the workflow is new. Steady beats a burst.
- Vertical video is 9:16 (1080 × 1920).

## Three examples (made-up businesses, never copy them)
- Marcus, the roofer: a reel standing in an attic with a flashlight, "Ten minutes up here tells you if that stain is new." A carousel: "Repair or replace? 5 photos that decide it."
- Priya, who coaches therapists: a carousel, "The 4 questions to answer before your first post." A reel answering one question she gets every week.
- Hannah, the candle maker: a reel of two candles side by side, one tunneled and one burned even. A carousel: "How to burn a candle so it lasts, in 5 steps."
