# YouTube playbook

Read by stand-up-a-specialist when the owner picked YouTube.

## Two different things live here
- **Shorts** (vertical, under a minute) work like reels: they reach strangers fast and are easy to make from the same one-take.
- **Long videos** (8 to 20 minutes) are found through search and suggested videos for months. They build the most trust of anything you can post, and they take the most work.

## What to post first
Start with Shorts cut from your one-takes, and plan ONE long video. A long video answers a question your buyer actually types into YouTube.

## The rules
- **The title and the thumbnail are the whole click.** Write five titles before you record, using the opener bank in write-hooks, and pick the one a buyer would search or click. The thumbnail shows one thing with three words at most, and it never repeats the title.
- **Say the payoff in the first 30 seconds,** then deliver it. People leave the moment they think it's not for them.
- **One video, one question.** If it answers two, it's two videos.
- **The description's first two lines** say what the video answers, in the words people search.
- One long video a month is a real habit for a business owner. Weekly is a job.

## Three examples (made-up businesses, never copy them)
- Marcus, the roofer: long video "Roof repair or replacement? How to tell before anyone quotes you." Shorts from the attic check.
- Priya, who coaches therapists: long video "Leaving agency work to open a private practice: the first 90 days." Shorts answering one question each.
- Hannah, the candle maker: Shorts of burn tests, and a long video "Why your candles tunnel, and how to fix every one you own."
