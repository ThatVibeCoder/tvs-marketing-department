# TikTok playbook

Read by stand-up-a-specialist when the owner picked TikTok.

## How it works
- **Everything goes to strangers first.** TikTok shows a new video to a small group of people who don't follow you, then to more if they watch. Your follower count matters less here than anywhere else, which is why a brand-new account can get seen.
- **Watching to the end is the signal.** A video people finish beats a polished one people skip.
- **Talking to the camera works.** A real person saying something useful or opinionated, straight to the phone, is the native format. Heavy editing isn't needed.

## What to post first
Three one-take videos from your script options (Day 12's workflow), each under 45 seconds, each opening on the claim in the first sentence.

## The rules
- **Say the whole pitch in the first sentence.** No "hey guys", no "in this video".
- **Talk to THEM:** "you" and "your", not "I" and "my".
- **Captions on screen,** because a lot of people watch with the sound off.
- **Reply to comments with a video** when a question deserves one. It's the easiest next post you'll ever get.
- Three a week is plenty while the workflow is new.

## Three examples (made-up businesses, never copy them)
- Marcus, the roofer: "If you've got a water stain after a storm, don't call anyone yet. Go up in the attic first..." (30 seconds).
- Priya, who coaches therapists: "Do you need a niche to open a private practice? Yes, and here's the 2-minute way to pick one..."
- Hannah, the candle maker: "Your candle isn't bad. You just burned it wrong the first time..."
