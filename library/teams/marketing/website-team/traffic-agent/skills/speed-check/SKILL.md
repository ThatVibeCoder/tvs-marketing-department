# speed-check

Slow pages lose the people the traffic work earned.

## Steps
1. Load the page fresh and honestly clock it. More than about three seconds on a phone connection is losing people.
2. Images are the usual thief: sized to what's displayed, compressed, no five-megabyte hero photos.
3. Fonts: the two brand fonts only, loaded once. Every extra font is a pause.
4. Report: FAST, or what's slow and the fix, in plain words.

## Rules
- Speed is a feature of every fix, not a project of its own. Catch it per page and it never becomes one.
