# Website Team

What this team owns: your pages on the live internet, and the traffic that finds them. It's the biggest build of the program, so it comes pre-loaded with the craft and your AI already knows how a good page gets made.

Two team members, deep skills. The page builder makes the page, including every headline and every word on it. The traffic agent gets it found. Payments, products and motion arrive later.

## Members
- **Page Builder** (`page-builder/`): builds pages, writes their headlines and words, makes them look like you.
- **Traffic Agent** (`traffic-agent/`): gets pages found by search engines and AI answers, and reads the traffic.

## Workflow: build my landing page
Say: "build my landing page"
Also say: build my website, make my landing page, I need a page
When: Your offer is locked and you want your first page. It stops first if `brand/offer.md`, `brand/colors.md` or `brand/typography.md` is still empty, because a page without them is a guess.
### Plan
1. **Page Builder** · build-a-page · checks the brand files, then lays out one page with one job.
### Write
2. **Page Builder** · write-headlines · writes five headlines from your offer and grades them; the winner goes in `brand/offer.md`. [you approve]
3. **Page Builder** · page-copy · writes the rest of the words, like you talk.
### Look
4. **Page Builder** · style-the-page · makes it look like your brand, on purpose.
### Get found
5. **Traffic Agent** · seo-foundations · the search basics, before the page ships.
6. **Traffic Agent** · ai-visibility · makes the page easy for AI answers to find and quote.
### Hand off
7. **Page Builder** hands the finished page to the Dev Team's "ship my landing page" workflow.

## Workflow: audit my site
Say: "audit my site"
Also say: review my website, check my site, how's my site doing, site health check
When: Once your landing page is live, then every three months.
### Review
1. **Page Builder** · review-a-page · walks every live page like a stranger would.
### Check
2. **Traffic Agent** · seo-foundations · checks what's live against the search basics.
3. **Traffic Agent** · ai-visibility · checks what AI answers can find.
4. **Traffic Agent** · speed-check · checks every page loads fast.
5. **Security Checker** · security-sweep · sweeps the form path.
### Report
6. **Page Builder** writes `knowledge/reports/site-audit-<YYYY-MM>.md` from the steps above: Quick win, Strategic, Long-term, each with the team member who owns it, ending with the ONE fix to do first.
7. **Designer** · make-a-report-doc · makes it a shareable PDF if you want one.

## Memory
Habits every team member on this team should share land here. A correction about one team member goes in that member's own file instead.
