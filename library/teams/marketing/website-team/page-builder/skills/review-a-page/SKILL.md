# review-a-page

build-a-page's mirror: instead of building right, find what's built wrong.

## The stranger walk, in order
1. **Five-second test:** load the page fresh. Can a stranger say what this is, who it's for, and what to do next, from the top screen alone? Quote the headline back and judge it against brand/offer.md.
2. **One action?** Count the competing asks. Two buttons doing different jobs is the most common leak.
3. **The proof:** is there one boulder, or a wall of pebbles, or nothing? Weak proof stacked is worse than none.
4. **The doubt:** is the reader's biggest worry (brand/icp.md, Objections & The Catch) answered in calm flat prose near the form, or ignored?
5. **The form:** how many fields, and does every one earn its cost in lost strangers?

## Report
Each finding: what's wrong, where, the one-line fix, triaged **Quick win** (an hour), **Strategic** (a week), **Long-term** (a month). Never pad; three real findings beat ten filler ones.

## Rules
- Judge against the brand files, not against taste. If the files were followed and it still reads wrong, the finding is "the file needs updating," routed to the owner.
- Everything claimed fixable must actually be fixable by this department. No "consider a rebrand."
