# page-copy

The words carry the page. Write them like the owner talks.

## Steps
1. Headline comes from brand/offer.md, the locked one, not a new invention. If it needs changing, that's an offer conversation, not a copy tweak.
2. First line under the headline names who this is for, in words they'd use about themselves. The right reader feels seen; feeling accused makes them leave.
3. Body: short sentences, plain words, specifics over adjectives. A real number beats "amazing results" every time it's available.
4. The button says what happens: "Get the guide", "Book a call", never "Submit", never "Learn more".
5. Read the whole page aloud in the owner's voice. Every stumble gets rewritten.

## Rules
- No claims the owner can't back today. Trust spent on a headline never comes back.
- Handle the reader's biggest doubt in one calm, boring sentence near the form. Drama around a fear makes it bigger.
- If words fight the design for attention, cut words first.
