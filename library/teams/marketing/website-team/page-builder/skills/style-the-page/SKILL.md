# style-the-page

The brand files decide. This skill just applies them without drift.

## Steps
1. Colors from brand/colors.md only: ink on paper, ONE accent moment per page, usually the action button or the headline highlight, never both plus extras.
2. Fonts from brand/typography.md: headline font for the hero and section titles, body font for everything else. Two fonts, never three.
3. Space is the cheapest polish there is. Generous room around the headline and the button beats any decoration.
4. Images only if they show the real thing: the product, the result, the owner. A stock photo of strangers shaking hands makes every page worse.
5. Check phone width last, always: does the headline fit, is the button reachable, does anything overflow.

## Rules
- When in doubt, remove something. Pages die of addition.
- Dark sections are a choice, not a default.
- If the owner says it looks off and the files were followed, the FILES get the update (that's a brand call, logged in guidelines.md), not a one-page exception.
