# Ads Team

What this team owns: paid traffic, when you're ready to pay for it. It gets set up after your pages and offer are proven without ads.

Three team members to start: the growth lead decides and reads the numbers, the ads manager runs the ad platform, the ad writer makes the versions.

## Members
- **Growth Lead** (`growth-lead/`): one recommendation instead of five dashboards.
- **Ads Manager** (`ads-manager/`): campaigns staged, nothing launched without your word.
- **Ad Writer** (`ad-writer/`): ad versions written from your offer and ICP.

## Workflows

## Workflow: launch my first ads
Say: "launch my first ads"
Also say: run ads, start facebook ads, set up meta ads, test an ad
When: Your offer is locked and your landing page is live and catching leads. It stops first if the ads-ready check says "not yet", because ads only make a broken page fail faster.
### Check
1. **Growth Lead** · ads-ready-check · confirms the offer, the live page, real customers without ads, and your target cost per lead. [you approve]
### Track
2. **Ads Manager** · set-up-tracking · the pixel and one event, proven with a test lead.
### Write
3. **Researcher** · competitor-check · what your competitors are running in Meta's Ad Library, and the gap nobody fills.
4. **Page Builder** · build-a-page · one page per promise: two or three pages, each locked to one angle from your ICP.
5. **Ad Writer** · write-ad-batch · three to five really different ads, each pointing at its own page. [you approve]
### Launch
6. **Growth Lead** · plan-a-test · your budget, the end date, and the winner and stop lines, written before anything runs. [you approve]
7. **Ads Manager** · build-paused-campaign · builds it paused, and you turn it on in Ads Manager yourself. [you approve]

## Workflow: read my ads
Say: "read my ads"
Also say: how are my ads doing, weekly ads report, check my ads
When: Once a week while a test is live.
### Read
1. **Growth Lead** · weekly-ads-read · the numbers against your plan, page problem or ad problem, and one recommendation.
### Act
2. **Ads Manager** · build-paused-campaign · drafts the pause or cut the read called for, and acts only on your yes. [you approve]
3. **Ad Writer** · iterate-a-winner · new versions of the winner for the next test, only if there is a winner. [you approve]
