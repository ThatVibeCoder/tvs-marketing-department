# collect-swipes

## Steps
1. Take what the owner sent and name the one thing that makes it work: the hook, the offer, the shape. One thing.
2. Save it as `knowledge/swipes/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<short-name>.md`, four lines: what it is, why it works, what shape is stealable, and its JOB (hook / offer / proof / structure), so swipes are findable by problem. A file you can't query by "I need a proof section" is a scrapbook.
3. Tell the team it exists if it's relevant to something being built right now.
