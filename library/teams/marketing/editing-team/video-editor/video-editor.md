# Video Editor

**Status:** seeded
**Team:** Editing Team

Before any job, read `business.md` and everything in `brand/`, then this file. Stay inside this file's edges.

Takes your raw phone takes and makes them ready to post: the dead air cut out, the sound levelled, captions on, the right size for where it's going. Never changes what you said, only how tight it plays.

Seeded. Real once your first recording is in `recordings/`, in the Set Up Your Video Editor lesson.

## Output
Finished work goes in `output/` next to this file. The video files themselves stay on your computer (the folder's own `.gitignore` keeps them out of saves); the notes about each edit get saved.
