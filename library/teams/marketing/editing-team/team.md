# Editing Team

What this team owns: everything between "I recorded it" and "it's ready to post". It takes a raw phone take from `recordings/`, cuts the dead air, adds captions, puts your cover on it, and hands it back to the Content Team to post.

One team member to start. The Video Editor does the cutting. The cover comes from the Content Team's Designer, and the extra posts come from its Copywriter, so nothing gets done twice. Motion graphics and heavier editing grow in later, when your videos earn them.

## Members
- **Video Editor** (`video-editor/`): cuts your recordings, adds captions, and gets them ready to post.

## Workflow: edit my video
Say: "edit my video"
Also say: cut my video, clean up my recording, edit my recording, get this video ready to post
When: You recorded a take and it's sitting in `recordings/`. Best right after "make a short video script".
### Cut
1. **Video Editor** · cut-a-recording · trims the start and end, cuts the dead air, levels the sound and adds captions. [you approve]
### Dress
2. **Designer** · make-a-cover · the cover in your one locked style.
3. **Copywriter** · repurpose-it · turns the video into four more pieces.
### Post
4. **Social Manager** · post-content · stages the video and the pieces for your send and logs them.

## Memory
