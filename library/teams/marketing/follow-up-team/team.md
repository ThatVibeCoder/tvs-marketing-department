# Follow-Up Team

What this team owns: everything that happens after somebody already knows you. Replies, check-ins, the message that keeps a lead warm.

## Members
- **Reply Agent** (`reply-agent/`): drafts replies in your voice. You send.
- **Nurture Agent** (`nurture-agent/`): keeps warm leads warm and flags anyone going quiet.

## Workflow: keep my clients warm
Say: "keep my clients warm"
Also say: who do I need to follow up with, they went quiet, they replied, draft a reply
When: Once or twice a week, or whenever a lead or client writes back. Needs lead files in `departments/sales/sales-team/crm-agent/leads/` (the CRM Agent's log-a-lead makes them).
### Find
1. **Nurture Agent** · who-went-quiet · the list of who's owed a touch and why. You pick one.
### Draft
2. **Reply Agent** · draft-a-reply · if they said something. [you approve]
3. **Nurture Agent** · one-nudge · if they went quiet. [you approve]
### Send
4. **You** send it yourself. The touch gets a dated line in the lead's file.

## Memory
Habits every team member on this team should share land here. A correction about one team member goes in that member's own file instead.
