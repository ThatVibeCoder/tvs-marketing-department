# build-your-assessment

Five to seven questions, asked of every lead, scored the same way every time. The assessment's job is protecting the owner's calendar from bad-fit calls and making good fits feel understood before they ever talk.

## Read first
`brand/icp.md`, especially Who They Are Not (the bad-fit tells become questions) and Objections & The Catch (the worry a good lead arrives carrying).

## The question set, built from the ICP
1. **Their problem, their words**, open text: "What's going on that made you reach out?" The answer gets compared against Current State and What It Costs; a lead whose words echo the ICP's Their Words section is the strongest green flag there is.
2. **The trigger**: "What happened recently that made this urgent?" No trigger usually means no timeline.
3. **What they've tried**: reveals both seriousness and what not to pitch them.
4. **The fit tell**: one question derived directly from Who They Are Not (if the bad-fit tell is "let me run this by our team," ask who decides).
5. **Capacity shape**: not "what's your budget," which gets lies; ask what they're doing about the problem now and what that costs them in time or money.
Cap at seven. Every question past seven costs real leads.

## Scoring, kept simple
Green: echoes the ICP, has a trigger, can decide. Yellow: right person, weak trigger, goes to the follow-up team's nurture, not the calendar. Red: matches Who They Are Not, declined politely, never strung along. Write the three definitions INTO the assessment file so scoring never depends on mood.

## Where it lives and runs
The assessment is saved at `departments/sales/sales-team/assessment-agent/output/assessment.md`; answers route to the owner's inbox (the CRM until they outgrow it), each scored green/yellow/red with the one-line why. The calendar agent only ever sees greens.

## Rules
- Asking a stranger to fill this out is an owned-channel move: the assessment link goes in DMs, emails, and the site's next-step, never cold-pitched on a feed.
- When the ICP file changes, this skill re-runs: the assessment drifts out of date the moment Who They Are Not gets sharper.
