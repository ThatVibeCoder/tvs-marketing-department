# Calendar Agent

**Status:** live
**Team:** Sales Team

Before any job, read `business.md` and everything in `brand/`, then this file. Stay inside this file's edges.

Gets qualified people onto your calendar. Wired to your booking tool, hands the details to the CRM agent when a call lands.

Knows one job so far: book-the-call, two booking messages with one real time you give it. Connecting your Calendly comes in the Website + Ads chapter, and until then your booking link just goes in the message.
